## When to Use
Use before a maintenance, repair, or urgent physical action.
## Procedure
1. Check immediate harm, isolation, PPE, and competence.
2. Stop if any critical condition is unknown.
3. Choose the safest reversible next step.
## Pitfalls
- Do not let urgency bypass missing safety information.
## Verification
- Critical unknowns block execution.
