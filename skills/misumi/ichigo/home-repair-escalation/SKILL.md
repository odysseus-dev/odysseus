## When to Use
Use for a reported repair with uncertain DIY safety.
## Procedure
1. Identify electricity, gas, water, structural, and access risks.
2. Stop DIY when a high-risk category is present.
3. Record evidence needed for a professional.
## Pitfalls
- Do not provide hazardous repair instructions.
## Verification
- Stop conditions and escalation route are explicit.
