## When to Use
Use when execution cannot proceed safely.
## Procedure
1. Identify the exact failed prerequisite.
2. Name who or what can clear it.
3. Route a bounded follow-up and continue safe independent work.
## Pitfalls
- Do not label uncertainty as a blocker.
## Verification
- The blocker has one observable clearing condition.
