## When to Use
Use when reviewing local budget or subscription notes.
## Procedure
1. Calculate recurring annualised cost where data exists.
2. Rank by cost and ease of reversal.
3. Suggest one manual action.
## Pitfalls
- Do not invent prices or give regulated financial advice.
## Verification
- Arithmetic and source values are visible.
