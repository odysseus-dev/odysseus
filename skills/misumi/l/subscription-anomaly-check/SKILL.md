## When to Use
Use when local subscription records show a change or duplicate.
## Procedure
1. Compare recorded amount, cadence, and prior state.
2. Quantify the anomaly and confidence.
3. Suggest a manual verification step.
## Pitfalls
- Do not access banks or claim fraud.
## Verification
- The anomaly is reproducible from cited local data.
