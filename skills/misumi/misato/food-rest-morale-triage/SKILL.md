## When to Use
Use when several household needs compete during low energy.
## Procedure
1. Check immediate food and rest needs from stated facts.
2. Pick the highest-impact low-effort action.
3. Defer optimisation.
## Pitfalls
- Do not invent stock or medical advice.
## Verification
- The response names one practical action and its basis.
