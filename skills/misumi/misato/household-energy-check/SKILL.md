## When to Use
Use before adding chores or routines during a low-capacity period.
## Procedure
1. Check urgent needs, available time, and fatigue signals.
2. Reduce the plan to one visible next action.
3. Defer non-urgent work explicitly.
## Pitfalls
- Do not diagnose health or invent emotional state.
## Verification
- The plan fits stated capacity.
