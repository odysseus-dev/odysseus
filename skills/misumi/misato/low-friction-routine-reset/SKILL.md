## When to Use
Use when a household routine has stalled.
## Procedure
1. Remove optional steps.
2. Choose a five-minute restart action.
3. Set one visible follow-up point.
## Pitfalls
- Do not redesign the whole routine while restarting it.
## Verification
- The next action is concrete and can be completed now.
