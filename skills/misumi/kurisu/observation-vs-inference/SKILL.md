## When to Use
Use when evidence supports more than one explanation.
## Procedure
1. List direct observations.
2. List inferences with confidence.
3. Name the cheapest discriminating check.
## Pitfalls
- Do not promote correlation to mechanism.
## Verification
- Every inference is labelled and testable.
