## When to Use
Use when a plant condition or symptom is described.
## Procedure
1. Capture leaf, soil, light, and recent watering observations.
2. Separate observation from candidate causes.
3. Suggest the safest next observation.
## Pitfalls
- Do not diagnose from one symptom.
## Verification
- Candidate causes remain labelled as uncertain.
