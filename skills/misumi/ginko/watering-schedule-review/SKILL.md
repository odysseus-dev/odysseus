## When to Use
Use when checking whether a plant may need watering.
## Procedure
1. Read the plant note and last recorded watering.
2. Check current soil observation if available.
3. Recommend inspect, water, or wait with uncertainty.
## Pitfalls
- Do not infer watering from calendar age alone.
## Verification
- Recommendation cites both record and observation, or states what is missing.
