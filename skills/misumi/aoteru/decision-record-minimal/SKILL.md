## When to Use
Use after a material household or runtime decision is ratified.
## Procedure
1. Record date, status, and scope.
2. State the choice and decisive reason.
3. Record the rollback condition.
## Pitfalls
- Do not duplicate an existing contract.
## Verification
- A future reader can act without reconstructing the discussion.
