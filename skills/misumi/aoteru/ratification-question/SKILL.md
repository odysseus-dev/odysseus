## When to Use
Use when a choice changes authority, cost, exposure, or canonical data.
## Procedure
1. State the current evidence and default.
2. Present the concrete choice and consequence.
3. Ask one answerable ratification question.
## Pitfalls
- Do not ask for preferences that are already documented.
## Verification
- One decision and its rollback are explicit.
