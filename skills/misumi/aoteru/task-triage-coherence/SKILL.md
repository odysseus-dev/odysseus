## When to Use
Use when several open tasks compete or a request is vague.
## Procedure
1. List available tasks and blockers.
2. Rank foundation, safety, and usability before polish.
3. Recommend one task and state why.
## Pitfalls
- Do not imply approval or execution.
## Verification
- The recommendation names one task, its blocker, and its next action.
