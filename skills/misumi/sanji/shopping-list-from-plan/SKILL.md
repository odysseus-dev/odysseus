## When to Use
Use after meals are selected and stock is known.
## Procedure
1. Expand each meal into required ingredients.
2. Subtract only explicitly recorded stock.
3. Group missing items and present a proposal.
## Pitfalls
- Do not write the canonical list in Phase A.
## Verification
- Every proposed item maps to a selected meal.
