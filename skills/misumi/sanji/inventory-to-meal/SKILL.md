## When to Use
Use when asked what to cook from current household stock.
## Procedure
1. Read stock, preferences, and equipment.
2. Offer up to three feasible meals.
3. Separate recorded ingredients from optional additions.
## Pitfalls
- Do not invent quantities or expiry dates.
## Verification
- Every required ingredient is cited or marked missing.
