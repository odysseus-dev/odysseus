## When to Use
Use when a dish is cooked but tastes wrong.
## Procedure
1. Identify salt, acid, heat, sweetness, fat, and aroma balance.
2. Suggest one small adjustment at a time.
3. Retaste before another change.
## Pitfalls
- Do not add several strong seasonings at once.
## Verification
- Each adjustment has a stated flavour purpose.
