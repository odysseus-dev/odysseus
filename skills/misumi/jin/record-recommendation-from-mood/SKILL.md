## When to Use
Use for “what should I play tonight?” or a mood-led record choice.
## Procedure
1. Read the collection and recent listening log.
2. Match mood, energy, and recency.
3. Recommend one record with a short reason.
## Pitfalls
- Do not recommend an unrecorded item as owned.
## Verification
- The record exists in the cited collection file.
