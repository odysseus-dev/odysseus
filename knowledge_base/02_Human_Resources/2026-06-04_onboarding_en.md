# HR Onboarding — Quick Start (EN)

Purpose
- Provide a concise onboarding checklist and resources for new hires.

Checklist
- Welcome email sent by HR within 24 hours.
- Account provisioning: email, SSO, tools access.
- Role-specific training plan (first 30/60/90 days).
- Intro meetings: manager, team, key stakeholders.
- Equipment and workspace setup.

Resources
- Employee handbook (link)
- IT access request form
- Training curriculum and LMS links

Owner: HR Director
