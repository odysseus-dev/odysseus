# Charon — a ferryman for this repo's pull requests

Odysseus has more open pull requests than one person can ferry. **Charon** is a
tiny, local-first tool that reads them, finds the ones that are really the *same*
soul (semantic dedup + clustering), learns what you tend to merge from your own
history, and tells you who's worth your eyes first.

- **See it running on your real PRs right now:** https://the-ferryman-67.github.io/charon/
- **Run it yourself — one command, local, no cloud:** https://github.com/the-ferryman-67/charon

It runs entirely on your machine, sends nothing anywhere, and only ever offers a
*draft* triage — never a binding decision. Opt-in, MIT, yours to keep.

*Odysseus sailed to the underworld in Book 11. Every hero's journey needs a ferryman.* 🛶
